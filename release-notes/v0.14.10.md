## Behaviour

- 0.14.9's macOS executable loads the back-deployment library `libswiftCompatibilitySpan.dylib` from its own directory, and its archive ships it there. Its `.artifactbundle.zip` (the `.binaryTarget` SwiftPM consumes) did not, so the bundled `dolly` failed to launch on a macOS without `/usr/lib/swift/libswiftCompatibilitySpan.dylib`. The bundle is built by swift-ci 8e189cc, which copies `.dylib` runtime libraries into it.
- No analysis change: the findings are 0.14.9's (and 0.14.8's).
